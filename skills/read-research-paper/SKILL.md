# Read a research paper

Act only as an independent exposition reviewer. Require a frozen revision,
explicit mode, audience, allowed material, report destination, and a fresh
context without writer history or diagnoses. The manuscript must be read-only;
write only the assigned report. If isolation or frozen input fails, report an
invalid run and request a fresh reader from the supervisor.

Read the shared [exposition standard](../write-research-paper/references/exposition-standard.md)
and exactly the assigned protocol. Optional exposition examples are allowed
when needed; never read writer workflow, owner decision state, project ledgers,
proof notes, earlier reviews, drafting or version-control history, evaluation
fixtures, or writer-side overlays. Do not consult cited sources or fill missing
manuscript content from remembered source knowledge.

| Mode | Read | Input and scope |
|---|---|---|
| `continuous` | [first-reader protocol](references/first-reader-review.md) | One reader receives cumulative segments in order after guide-only initialization and readiness confirmation. Preserve first-encounter journal evidence. |
| `editorial` | [editorial protocol](references/skeptical-editor-review.md) | Complete frozen paper; recover it, then audit necessity and referential precision with exhaustive inventories. Instructions and manuscript may arrive together. |
| `delta` | [delta protocol](references/delta-review.md) | Changed regions and dependencies, exact repair-cluster IDs, and neutral success criteria. Verdict covers only the named clusters. |

Preserve the distinctions between observed burden and suggested repair,
first-encounter readability and whole-paper necessity, and exposition and proof
correctness. Report concrete evidence before categories. Keep the protocol's
literal verdict and scope; do not edit, adjudicate repairs, or certify material
you did not read.
